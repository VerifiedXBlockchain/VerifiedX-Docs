## Delivered

Items from the previous roadmap that have shipped and can be verified from public sources:

- **Prism documentation published.** The architecture one-sheet, explainer, FAQ, technical paper, and Bitcoin privacy matrix are on the [Prism Privacy](/docs/introduction/prism-privacy) page.
- **vBTC V2 specification and audit published.** The [vBTC V2 Technical Specification](/docs/documents/vbtc-technical) and the [Halborn vBTC v2 audit](/docs/documents/halborn-audit) are on this site.
